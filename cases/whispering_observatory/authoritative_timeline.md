# Authoritative Timeline: The Whispering Observatory

This timeline represents the objective truth of the case. It serves as the master reference for validating suspect testimony and identifying contradictions.

### event_01: Guest Arrival
*   **Time:** 48 hours prior to murder
*   **Location:** Observatory Arrival Deck / Main Entrance
*   **Participants:** Alistair Thorne (victim_01), all suspects (suspect_01–06)
*   **Objective Truth:** The guests arrive via the mountain lift before the storm cycle began. Alistair welcomes the group. Marcus Thorne arrives unannounced, causing immediate visible friction.
*   **Witnesses:** All characters.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** Facility visitor logs; external security footage (now offline).
*   **Uncertainty:** None.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Marcus claims he was invited by his father to discuss a settlement; Alistair’s private notes indicate Marcus was never on the guest list.

### event_02: The Discovery Announcement
*   **Time:** 24 hours prior to murder
*   **Location:** location_01 (The Great Reflector Dome)
*   **Participants:** All characters
*   **Objective Truth:** Alistair Thorne gives a formal presentation of the discovery. He deliberately omits Dr. Elena Vance's name from the credits and slides. Elena remains silent but is observed by Hugo Sterling looking "unnaturally still."
*   **Witnesses:** All suspects.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** Digital presentation files; printed gala programs.
*   **Uncertainty:** The exact degree of Elena's technical contribution is public knowledge, but the depth of her resentment is hidden.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Elena later claims she was "proud of Alistair's success" and didn't mind the lack of credit.

### event_03: The Storm Begins / The Key Theft
*   **Time:** 11:00 AM (12 hours prior to murder)
*   **Location:** location_08 (Catwalk) / location_05 (East Wing Quarters)
*   **Participants:** suspect_05 (Silas Reed), suspect_01 (Elena Vance)
*   **Objective Truth:** The snowstorm intensifies. Silas Reed performs a final perimeter sweep on the Outdoor Maintenance Catwalk (location_08). While he is outside, Elena enters Silas's security workspace (a sub-area within his quarters at location_05) and steals clue_07 (Master Key).
*   **Witnesses:** None.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_02 (Logbook entry for 11:00 is smudged/altered by Silas); clue_07 (Master Key missing from the security dock).
*   **Uncertainty:** Silas initially believes he simply misplaced the key during the storm sweep.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Silas omits the loss of the key from his morning report; Elena never admits to entering his workspace.

### event_04: Murder Window Start
*   **Time:** 22:00
*   **Location:** location_03 (Common Area) / location_01 (Dome)
*   **Participants:** victim_01, suspect_03 (Suna), suspect_04 (Julian)
*   **Objective Truth:** Alistair announces he is going to the dome for "final lens calibrations" before the midnight data uplink. He appears manic and dismissive of others.
*   **Witnesses:** Suna Aksoy and Julian Vane observe him leaving.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** Internal movement sensors (logged before the flicker).
*   **Uncertainty:** None.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** None.

### event_05: The West Wing Argument
*   **Time:** 22:15
*   **Location:** location_04 (West Wing Corridor)
*   **Participants:** victim_01, suspect_02 (Marcus)
*   **Objective Truth:** Marcus intercepts Alistair. They have a violent verbal confrontation regarding Marcus's mother and the 1998 expedition. Alistair physically shoves Marcus before continuing toward the dome.
*   **Witnesses:** Hugo Sterling (observes from his doorway); Julian Vane (records the audio).
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_16 (Julian’s audio recording).
*   **Uncertainty:** The exact physical extent of the "shove" is disputed between Marcus and Hugo.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Marcus initially claims he was in his room the entire night; Alistair’s notes omit the encounter.

### event_06: The Power Flicker
*   **Time:** 22:45
*   **Location:** Facility-wide / location_06 (Server Room)
*   **Participants:** suspect_01 (Elena), suspect_05 (Silas)
*   **Objective Truth:** Elena Vance triggers a localized power flicker from the Server Room (location_06) to mask her entry into Service Shaft C. During the flicker, she also cuts the emergency radio wires (clue_03). Silas Reed is occupied at the main breakers in the East Wing.
*   **Witnesses:** All characters experience the flicker.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_03 (Disconnected emergency radio wires).
*   **Uncertainty:** Most characters assume the flicker was caused by a lightning strike or storm surge.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Elena claims she was in the Mess Hall making tea during the flicker.

### event_07: The Murder
*   **Time:** 23:00 - 23:10
*   **Location:** location_08 (Outdoor Maintenance Catwalk) / location_02 (Control Room)
*   **Participants:** victim_01, suspect_01 (Elena)
*   **Objective Truth:** At 23:00, a brief lull in the storm occurs, clearing visibility momentarily. Alistair Thorne exits the dome (location_01) via the airlock onto the catwalk (location_08). Elena Vance, already present via Shaft C, pushes him through the open telescope aperture. He falls 25 meters to the dome floor. Elena retreats via Shaft C. At 23:08, she enters the Control Room (location_02) and executes a legacy software command to engage the internal dome bolt remotely.
*   **Witnesses:** Hugo Sterling hears a "fading shout" from his room at 23:00.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_17 (The footprints were created in fresh snow during the struggle and remained identifiable afterward); clue_10 (Elena’s damp coat); clue_09 (The scream heard by Hugo); clue_18 (Hugo’s false weather testimony regarding a whiteout at 23:00); clue_11 (Data logs of the 23:08 bolt command).
*   **Uncertainty:** None regarding the act; only the interpretation of the locked door by investigators.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Elena claims she was in her quarters; Hugo Sterling falsely claims there was a total whiteout at 23:00 to provide an alibi for Elena.

### event_08: Discovery of the Body
*   **Time:** 23:30
*   **Location:** location_01 (The Great Reflector Dome)
*   **Participants:** suspect_03 (Suna), suspect_05 (Silas), suspect_01 (Elena)
*   **Objective Truth:** Suna Aksoy arrives at the dome and finds the door bolted from the inside. She finds Silas Reed, who uses the emergency hydraulic override to force the door. They discover Alistair's body. Elena arrives shortly after.
*   **Witnesses:** Suna, Silas, Elena, and later the other guests.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_15 (Locked door testimony); clue_05 (Suna’s medicine vial found at the scene).
*   **Uncertainty:** Whether the door was bolted manually or electronically is initially unknown to everyone except the killer.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Suna claims Alistair "called her" to deliver medicine; in reality, she went to the dome unprompted out of anxiety.

### event_09: The Lockdown
*   **Time:** 23:45
*   **Location:** Facility-wide
*   **Participants:** All characters
*   **Objective Truth:** Silas Reed declares a Level 4 Lockdown. Julian Vane attempts to use his satellite phone but finds the uplink blocked by the storm.
*   **Witnesses:** All suspects.
*   **Supporting Evidence:** clue_08 (Julian’s hidden phone).
*   **Uncertainty:** The guests are unaware that the radio has been sabotaged.
*   **Lies/Omissions:** Julian claims he has no way to contact the outside world.
