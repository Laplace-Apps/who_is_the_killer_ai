*   **Case ID:** CASE-2026-V-001

*   **Setting:** The Andean Peaks Observatory, a high-altitude research facility isolated by a severe mountain snowstorm
*   **Time Period:** Late Autumn, Present Day

*   **Player Objective:** Identify the killer of Dr. Alistair Thorne using testimony, physical evidence, and logical deduction

*   **Themes:** Ambition, legacy, isolation, and the weight of past secrets

### Premise
At the remote Andean Peaks Observatory, thousands of meters above civilization, a celebration for a breakthrough discovery turns into a nightmare. As a sudden, violent snowstorm severs the only access road and disables long-range communications, the facility's director, Dr. Alistair Thorne, is found dead at the base of the Great Reflector telescope. The dome was locked from the inside, yet the fall that killed him was physically impossible from the telescope's observation deck. As an independent detective caught in the storm, you must navigate a web of professional jealousy, family resentment, and hidden identities among the six remaining guests. Every suspect has a motive; only one is a killer.
