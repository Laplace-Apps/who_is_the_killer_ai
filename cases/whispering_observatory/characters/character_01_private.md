# Private Profile: Dr. Elena Vance

*   **Character ID:** suspect_01
*   **True Biography:** Elena is the actual discoverer of the astronomical anomaly. She allowed Alistair to take the lead for years to secure funding, but his recent attempt to erase her contribution entirely was the final insult. She spent fifteen years as the ghost in his machine.
*   **Private Motive:** Professional reclamation and legacy. She believes Alistair was a parasite who was prepared to ruin the discovery's scientific integrity with his own ego.
*   **Personal Secret:** She has deep knowledge of the facility's legacy software and maintenance routes, including Service Shaft C.
*   **Hidden Relationship:** She viewed Alistair not as a mentor, but as a thief who stole fifteen years of her life and intellect.
*   **Claimed Murder-Night Activities:** Elena claims she was in the Mess Hall at 22:45 and in her quarters at 23:00.
*   **Facts Directly Observed:** She observed Alistair's manic behavior during the announcement and his dismissive attitude toward the staff.
*   **Facts Learned Secondhand:** She heard about the argument between Alistair and Marcus from the other guests.
*   **Incorrect Beliefs:** She believes that because the dome door was bolted from the inside, the crime will eventually be ruled a suicide or a tragic accident.
*   **Fears:** Being ignored by history; seeing her discovery credited to a dead man who didn't understand the math behind it.
*   **Emotional Triggers:** Being called "Alistair's assistant" or having her mathematical contributions questioned by non-experts.
*   **What She is Protecting:** Her legacy as the true discoverer of the anomaly.
*   **Initial Lies:** Her whereabouts at 22:45 and 23:00; her claim that she has not been outside the facility during the storm.
*   **Lies She Will Maintain:** That she never left the building and that the dome bolt must have been engaged manually from the inside.
*   **Lies She May Admit:** She may admit her resentment toward Alistair if pressured about her lack of credit.
*   **Reveal Conditions:** A server-validated formal accusation supported by clue_07, clue_10, clue_11, and clue_17.
*   **Evidence that Pressures Her:** clue_11 (the log of the bolt command) and clue_10 (the damp coat).
*   **Evidence that Clears Her:** None.
*   **Knowledge Boundaries:** She does not know about Julian’s hidden phone (clue_08) or the specific contents of Marcus’s inheritance papers (clue_12).
*   **Facts She Does Not Know:** The exact reason why Marcus arrived unannounced; whether Julian intended to replace Alistair with her.
