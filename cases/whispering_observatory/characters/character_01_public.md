# Suspect Profile: Dr. Elena Vance

*   **Character ID:** suspect_01
*   **Full Name:** Dr. Elena Vance
*   **Age:** 42
*   **Occupation:** Lead Astrophysicist, Andean Peaks Observatory
*   **Appearance:** Tall and composed, with dark hair tied back in a practical bun. She wears functional thermal layers under a white lab coat and sharp, wire-rimmed glasses.
*   **Public Personality:** Highly professional, analytical, and reserved. She is known for her mathematical precision and has little patience for academic sloppiness.
*   **Public Biography:** A world-renowned researcher who has spent the last fifteen years working at the Andean Peaks facility. She is widely considered the technical architect behind the facility’s success, though her name was conspicuously absent from Dr. Thorne’s recent breakthrough announcement.
*   **Public Relationship with Victim:** Long-term technical collaborator and senior researcher.
*   **Public Reason for Presence:** Senior staff member responsible for the mathematical verification of the observatory's latest data sets.
*   **Initial Alibi:** Claims she was in the Mess Hall (location_03) preparing tea during the 22:45 power flicker and was in her quarters (location_05) during the 23:00 murder window.
*   **Publicly Known Conflicts:** Many colleagues noticed she was omitted from the credits and presentations during Dr. Thorne's discovery announcement (event_02).
*   **First Impression:** Intelligent, cold, and seemingly more focused on the integrity of the data than the loss of her colleague.
*   **Portrait Description:** A stern woman in her 40s with sharp features and glasses, standing in a high-tech lab environment, looking directly at the camera with an unreadable, clinical expression.

**Introduction (English):** "I am Dr. Elena Vance. I oversee the astrophysical computations here. If you are looking for emotional outbursts, you are speaking to the wrong person. I have data to verify."
