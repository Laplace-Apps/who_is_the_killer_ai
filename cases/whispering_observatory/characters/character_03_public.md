# Suspect Profile: Suna Aksoy

*   **Character ID:** suspect_03
*   **Full Name:** Suna Aksoy
*   **Age:** 28
*   **Occupation:** Postdoctoral Researcher, Andean Peaks Observatory
*   **Appearance:** Slight build, often seen in an oversized navy-blue observatory sweater. She wears thick-rimmed glasses and has her hair tied in a messy bun. Her hands are often stained with ink or graphite.
*   **Public Personality:** Intelligent, hardworking, and deeply respectful of academic hierarchy. She is notably soft-spoken and appears chronically sleep-deprived.
*   **Public Biography:** A brilliant graduate of Istanbul Technical University, Suna won a prestigious international fellowship to assist Dr. Thorne with the "Great Reflector" survey. She is considered a rising star in the field.
*   **Public Relationship with Victim:** Research assistant and protégé. She often speaks of Dr. Thorne with reverence.
*   **Public Reason for Presence:** Member of the core research team responsible for data collection.
*   **Initial Alibi:** Claims she was in her quarters (location_05) until 23:20, at which point she claims Dr. Thorne called her to bring his high-altitude medicine to the Dome.
*   **Publicly Known Conflicts:** None. She is generally seen as the "perfect student."
*   **First Impression:** Anxious and fragile. She seems overwhelmed by the death and the presence of the authorities.
*   **Portrait Description:** A young Turkish woman with glasses and an observatory sweater, looking nervous and clutching a notebook, set against a background of computer monitors and star charts.

**Introduction (English):** "I’m Suna Aksoy. I... I was just his assistant. I still can't believe he's gone. The research was everything to him."

**Introduction (Turkish):** "Ben Suna Aksoy. Ben... sadece onun asistanıydım. Hala öldüğüne inanamıyorum. Araştırma onun için her şeydi."
