# Private Profile: Julian Vane

*   **Character ID:** suspect_04
*   **True Biography:** Julian is not just a benefactor; he is a man on the brink. Vane Dynamics is facing a catastrophic shortfall, and he desperately needs a "clean" scientific miracle to stabilize his board of directors.
*   **Private Motive:** Corporate survival. He needed Alistair to be the perfect face of the discovery. If Alistair were exposed as a fraud or a tyrant, the funding—and Julian’s empire—would collapse.
*   **Personal Secret:** He possesses a working, encrypted satellite phone (clue_08) despite telling the detective all communications are down. He also recorded the 22:15 argument between Alistair and Marcus on his personal device (clue_16).
*   **Hidden Relationship:** He was secretly negotiating with Dr. Elena Vance to take over the project if Alistair’s erratic behavior became a liability.
*   **Claimed Murder-Night Activities:**
    *   22:00 – 23:45: Claims he remained in his suite (location_04) in the West Wing, working on "sensitive corporate matters."
*   **Facts Directly Observed:** He heard the loud argument between Marcus and Alistair at 22:15 while walking back to his suite. He used his phone to record it, thinking it might be useful leverage.
*   **Facts Learned Secondhand:** He knows Suna Aksoy is terrified of Alistair, as he overheard a sharp rebuke earlier in the day.
*   **Incorrect Beliefs:** He suspects Silas Reed might be more incompetent than he lets on and perhaps let an outsider into the facility.
*   **Fears:** The "Thorne Discovery" being debunked or mired in a murder scandal, leading to the bankruptcy of Vane Dynamics.
*   **Emotional Triggers:** Questions about his company’s solvency; being treated like a "bank" rather than a visionary.
*   **What He is Protecting:** His corporate reputation and the market value of the discovery.
*   **Initial Lies:** Claims he has no way to contact the outside world.
*   **Lies He Will Maintain:** He will never reveal his secret negotiations with Elena Vance unless cornered.
*   **Lies He May Admit:** Having the satellite phone (contradiction_07) once the device is found.
*   **Reveal Conditions:** Discovery of clue_08 (satellite phone) or clue_16 (the recording).
*   **Evidence that Pressures Him:** clue_08 (Satellite phone), clue_16 (Argument recording).
*   **Evidence that Clears Him:** No evidence connects Julian to the master key, the maintenance route, or the 23:08 Control Room command.
*   **Knowledge Boundaries:** He does not know who committed the murder, who stole the master key, or how the locked-room effect was created.
*   **Facts He Does Not Know:** He is unaware that Service Shaft C exists or that a remote bolt command was issued at 23:08.
