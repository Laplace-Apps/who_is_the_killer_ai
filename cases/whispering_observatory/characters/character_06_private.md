# Private Profile: Dr. Hugo Sterling

*   **Character ID:** suspect_06
*   **True Biography:** Hugo is a man haunted by a decision he made in 1998. During an expedition, he and Alistair covered up a fatal accident involving a junior researcher to prevent the facility's funding from being pulled. Alistair has used this shared secret to keep Hugo in his orbit for decades.
*   **Private Motive:** Protection of a legacy. He wants the observatory—and those he cares about, particularly Elena Vance—to thrive, but he feared Alistair’s ego was beginning to expose their old secrets.
*   **Personal Secret:** The 1998 cover-up (clue_04). He also suffers from psychosomatic trembling (clue_13) when the victim or that year is mentioned.
*   **Hidden Relationship:** He feels a fatherly protection toward Elena Vance, knowing how much Alistair has stolen from her intellectually. He suspects Alistair was planning to eventually cast her aside.
*   **Claimed Murder-Night Activities:**
    *   22:00 – 23:45: Claims he was in his room (location_04) and saw "nothing but white" through his window.
*   **Facts Directly Observed:** He looked out his window at exactly 23:00 and saw that the storm had actually lulled, offering clear visibility for a few minutes. He also heard Alistair's scream.
*   **Facts Learned Secondhand:** He overheard Marcus and Alistair's argument at 22:15.
*   **Incorrect Beliefs:** He believes Suna Aksoy is too weak to handle the pressure and might confess to things she hasn't done.
*   **Fears:** The 1998 secret being revealed; Elena Vance being unfairly blamed for the murder.
*   **Emotional Triggers:** Mention of the 1998 expedition; seeing the torn photo (clue_04).
*   **What He is Protecting:** The reputation of the observatory and Elena’s future.
*   **Initial Lies:** Claims it was a "total whiteout" at 23:00 (contradiction_06).
*   **Lies He Will Maintain:** He will never reveal the full details of the 1998 accident unless the detective presents the torn photo.
*   **Lies He May Admit:** That he heard the scream at 23:00 and that the weather wasn't as bad as he claimed.
*   **Reveal Conditions:** Confrontation with clue_18 (Weather logs) for contradiction_06. Confrontation with clue_04/clue_13 for the 1998 secret is INFERENCE_ONLY and never triggers Cornered/Cooperative.
*   **Evidence that Pressures Him:** clue_04 (Torn photo), clue_13 (Trembling hands), clue_18 (Weather logs).
*   **Evidence that Clears Him:** His physical frailty makes climbing maintenance shafts or a violent struggle on the catwalk highly implausible, and no evidence places him outdoors at 23:00.
*   **Knowledge Boundaries:** He does not know who committed the murder. He may fear Elena could be blamed, but he does not know the method, route, or cover-up.
*   **Facts He Does Not Know:** He does not know who took the master key, whether Shaft C was used, or that a remote bolt command was issued at 23:08.
