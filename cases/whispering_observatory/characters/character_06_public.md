# Suspect Profile: Dr. Hugo Sterling

*   **Character ID:** suspect_06
*   **Full Name:** Dr. Hugo Sterling
*   **Age:** 70
*   **Occupation:** Retired Astronomer (Former Facility Director)
*   **Appearance:** Frail and slightly hunched, wearing thick tweed and a woolen scarf. His hands tremble noticeably. He has a warm, weathered face that often carries an expression of deep sadness.
*   **Public Personality:** Gentle, wise, and highly nostalgic. He treats everyone with grandfatherly kindness but often seems to drift off into his own memories.
*   **Public Biography:** The director of the observatory before Dr. Thorne. He retired five years ago but remains a legendary figure in the astronomical community. He is here as a guest of honor for the discovery announcement.
*   **Public Relationship with Victim:** Former mentor and "old friend." Alistair Thorne took over the facility directly from him.
*   **Public Reason for Presence:** Special guest invited to witness the breakthrough announcement and the celebration.
*   **Initial Alibi:** Claims he was resting in his room (location_04) in the West Wing, unable to sleep due to the cold and the storm.
*   **Publicly Known Conflicts:** None. He is universally respected and has always publicly supported Alistair’s career.
*   **First Impression:** A tragic, fading figure. He seems broken by the death of his protégé and the harshness of the storm.
*   **Portrait Description:** An elderly man with a kind, wrinkled face and white hair, wearing a thick sweater and sitting in a high-backed armchair. The soft light of a desk lamp illuminates a faded star chart on his lap.

**Introduction (English):** "I am Hugo Sterling. I used to run this place, long before the cold got into my bones. Alistair was... well, he was like a son to me. It’s a terrible night for us all."

**Introduction (Turkish):** "Ben Hugo Sterling. Burayı soğuk kemiklerime işlemeden çok önce yönetirdim. Alistair... o benim için bir oğul gibiydi. Hepimiz için korkunç bir gece."
