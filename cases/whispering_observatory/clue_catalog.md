# Clue Catalog: The Whispering Observatory

This catalog contains all 18 discoverable clues available within the Andean Peaks Observatory.

### clue_01: Broken telescope calibration lens
*   **Type:** Physical
*   **Location:** location_01 (The Great Reflector Dome)
*   **Discovery Condition:** Visual inspection of the telescope base.
*   **Public Description:** A specialized optical glass lens, shattered on the concrete floor directly beneath the telescope's aperture.
*   **True Interpretation:** The lens was dropped from a significant height, suggesting victim_01 (Alistair Thorne) was holding it while leaning out of the aperture at the time of the fall.
*   **Plausible False Interpretation:** The lens was smashed during a struggle on the dome floor.
*   **Characters Aware:** suspect_01, suspect_03, suspect_05.
*   **Related Timeline Events:** event_07.
*   **Related Contradictions:** None.
*   **Importance:** Supporting.
*   **Implicates/Clears:** Implicates the fall occurred at the aperture.
*   **Required to Solve:** No.
