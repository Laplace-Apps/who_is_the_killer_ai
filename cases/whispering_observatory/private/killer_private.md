# Killer’s Secret Dossier: Dr. Elena Vance

### Killer Identity
*   **ID:** suspect_01
*   **Name:** Dr. Elena Vance

### True Motive
Dr. Alistair Thorne built his legacy on Elena's mathematical labor for fifteen years. The "Thorne Discovery" announcement (`event_02`) was the final betrayal, as he systematically scrubbed her name from the credits. Elena viewed the murder as a "reclamation of property." If she was to be silenced professionally, Alistair would be silenced permanently.

### Operational Timeline & Method
1.  **Preparation (11:00 AM):** While Captain Silas Reed was performing a perimeter sweep on the maintenance catwalk (`location_08`), Elena entered his workspace in the East Wing quarters (`location_05`) and stole the master maintenance key (`clue_07`).
2.  **Isolation (22:45):** Elena went to the Server Room (`location_06`). She triggered a power flicker to mask her movements and severed the radio wires (`clue_03`), ensuring the facility was isolated.
3.  **The Ascent:** Using the stolen key, she entered **Service Shaft C** inside `location_06`. This restricted maintenance shaft provided a direct, unmonitored vertical route to the Outdoor Maintenance Catwalk (`location_08`).
4.  **The Murder (23:00):** Elena waited on the catwalk for Alistair. During a brief lull in the storm, Alistair stepped out of the dome airlock. Elena pushed him through the open telescope aperture. He fell 25 meters to the dome floor (`location_01`).
5.  **The Impossible Crime (23:08):** Elena retreated via Shaft C and entered the Central Control Room (`location_02`). She executed a legacy "Emergency Atmospheric Bolt" command to remotely engage the mechanical internal bolt of the dome door. This created the "locked room" illusion to suggest Alistair was alone.

### Strategy and Lies
*   **The Alibi:** Elena claims she was in the Mess Hall (`location_03`) during the power flicker and in her quarters during the murder.
*   **The "Impossible" Defense:** She emphasizes that the door was "bolted from the inside" (`clue_15`) to steer the detective toward an accidental fall or suicide.
*   **Misdirection:** She encourages the detective to focus on Marcus Thorne’s documented aggression (`event_05`) or Suna Aksoy’s nervous behavior regarding the telescope (`clue_06`).

### Reaction to Critical Clues
*   **clue_10 (Damp Coat):** Elena will dismiss this as condensation or a leak in the East Wing lockers.
*   **clue_11 (Data Logs):** If confronted with the 23:08 bolt command (`contradiction_08`), she will claim it was a "ghost command" or a system glitch caused by the earlier power flicker.
*   **clue_17 (Footprints):** She knows these are hers, but she will correctly point out that the prints only prove a second person wore facility-issued boots and do not uniquely identify her.
*   **clue_10 and clue_17:** She acknowledges these implicate her in being outdoors, but she will maintain that being outdoors does not prove she pushed Alistair.

### Confession Conditions
Elena Vance is cold and disciplined. She will never volunteer a confession. She will only admit to the crime after the server validates a successful formal accusation that presents the complete logical evidence chain.
